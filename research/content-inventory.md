## Blocks
- Sizes AECT machines make: 4x12, 6x12, 6x12 holey, 7x14, 7x14 interlocking holey, 8x14, 10x14 (thickness 2–4.5", interlocking 3–5").
- Un-stabilized = soil only; needs roof overhang or coating (lime, stucco, paint). Can go on the wall right out of the machine.
- Stabilized = ~92% soil + ~8% cement/lime/fly ash; water resistant; must cure.
- AECT does not sell blocks directly on the site today. "Purchase CEBs" page points to partners: Colorado Earth (Denver CO), Anteros Earth Block (Baja California Sur, MX), Parabellum Constructors (Burnet TX). **But the calculator prices "Buy cement-stabilized CEBs from AECT, shipped to your state." ## FAQ facts (keep, they answer the repeat questions)
- Compression ~1,000–1,900 PSI.
- ~70% of earth works. Requirements: no organics (dig 12"+ down); need clay. Un-stabilized/lime: 20–35% clay. Cement-stabilized: 15–25% clay, more sand.
- Moisture 5–7%.
- A mixer is needed to get full machine capacity.
- Walls installed for < $20 per sq ft of home (material + labor). Walls are ~15% of build cost.
- Basements OK with cement-stabilized + moisture barrier.
- Electrical: surface mount, double wall with cavity, or grind a chaseway and plaster over.
- Wood attachment: Wall Dog screws or masonry anchors.
