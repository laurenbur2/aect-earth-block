Over the course of five months, with a small (3 members on an average day) crew, AECT built a beautiful 1,000 square foot CEB home using AECT compressed earth block machines! You can watch the entire 40-minute episode on the DIY Network website here:https://www.diynetwork.com/shows/building-off-the-grid-1/episodes/300/botg-earth-block-ranchThe team started by manufacturing the 9,500 CEBs needed to complete the build. The 6,500 exterior earth blocks were stabilized with approximately 8% portland cement…while the 3,000 interior earth blocks were un-stabilized.     AECT 2500 CEB machine at workWe used an AECT 2500 compressed earth block machine to make several thousand CEBs for this house build.  Find out more information about the AECT 2500 machine here:  https://aectearthblock.com/2500hd-series    Nice looking stack of 6" x 12" x 3.5" cement stabilized CEBs  The stabilized CEBs were laid on a rubble-trench foundation and kept plumb and level using story poles.   First course of compressed earth blocks   Story poles set up on all corners to help keep earth blocks straight   Window and door frames in place   Exterior CEB walls complete and roof going on  One last push of un-stabilized compressed earth blocks for the interior walls.  These CEBs were made using an AECT Impact 2001A compressed earth block machine.   Ryan with a conveyor full of compressed earth blocks fresh out of the AECT 2001A Impact CEB machine   Un-stabilized CEBs can go right onto the wall as soon as they come out of the earth block machine   The interior earth block walls are complete and ready for the American Clay finish   Ryan applying American Clay to the earthen wallsA scratch coat of earthen material was applied to the CEB walls and allowed to dry before applying the American Clay finish coat.  This wall covering is all-natural, breathable, and beautiful!     The American Clay finish looks amazing!
