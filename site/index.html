Quick answers

Questions we hear every day

Will my dirt work?

Probably. You need some clay (about 15–35%) and no topsoil. Dig 12" down and test it. Our Small Block Press makes test blocks at real machine pressure.

How much do CEB walls cost?

Usually under $20 per square foot of home, installed. Walls are only about 15% of a home's total cost. Get your exact numbers →

Which machine do I need?

Your block size decides it. The planner picks the right one for you, or browse all machines.

Stabilized or un-stabilized blocks?

If the wall will see rain, choose stabilized (about 8% cement). Pure-earth blocks are cheaper but need a roof overhang or plaster to stay dry.
