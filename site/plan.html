Will your exterior walls face the weather?

This decides whether you need stabilized (cement or lime added) or un-stabilized (pure earth) blocks.

And your interior walls?

Which one is right for me?

Many AECT customers use stabilized blocks on the outside and un-stabilized blocks inside. Un-stabilized walls must be kept dry: think wide porch roofs, or a lime, earthen or stucco finish. If you're unsure, choose stabilized; it's the safer default.
