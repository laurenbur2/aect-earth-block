Rental questions

Before you ask

Is renting cheaper than buying a machine?

For a single building, usually yes. For multiple buildings, or if you plan to sell blocks, owning a machine almost always wins. The planner compares both using your numbers.

How fast will my blocks be made?

Once supporting equipment, materials and crew are in place, AECT typically makes 5,000 to 8,000 10"×14"×3.5" blocks per week at your site.

What block sizes can I get with a rental?

Our past rental deployments have run the 3500, which makes 10"×14" blocks as standard, with optional 7"×14" and 8"×14" plates. Need 6"×12" or interlocking blocks? Ask us.

Do I need to test my soil first?

Yes. It's the most important step. Too much clay (over ~35%) causes cracking and lamination. We'll help you test and, if needed, find an amendment like sand or screenings.
